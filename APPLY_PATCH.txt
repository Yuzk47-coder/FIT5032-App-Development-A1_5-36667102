A3 D.2 EmailJS Patch

Apply this patch on top of your current FIX2 project.
Copy everything in this folder into the A3_SilverAge_WebApp root and choose Replace when Windows asks.

IMPORTANT:
- This patch does NOT include or overwrite your local .env file.
- Your Firebase configuration in .env is preserved.
- After copying, add the three VITE_EMAILJS_* lines to your existing .env manually.
